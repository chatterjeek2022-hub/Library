📱 Switching Phones
On your old phone — export and save the file to Google Drive or iCloud Drive.
On your new phone — open this HTML file in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
Go to Backup → Import and choose the file from your cloud storage.
All your books appear instantly. Done.
💡 This app runs identically on Android and iPhone — same HTML file, same data format, no app store needed.
